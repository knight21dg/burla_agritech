## 10. Policies — legally significant

Four pages. These must reflect **actual business practice**, not a template.

| Page | Must state |
|---|---|
| **Return & Refund** | Window, conditions, who pays return shipping, refund method and timeline, what is non-returnable (food often is) |
| **Delivery** | Courier partners, regions served, dispatch time, delivery estimates, charges, tracking |
| **Privacy** | What data is collected, why, retention, sharing, user rights, contact for requests (DPDP Act 2023) |
| **Terms & Conditions** | Entity, use of site, orders, pricing, liability, governing law and jurisdiction |

We can supply a structured draft. **The client is responsible for having them reviewed** — a copied policy that does not match actual practice is worse than none.
